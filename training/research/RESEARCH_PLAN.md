# Training v2 evidence audit

**Status: In progress — NOT a release.** Existing 100 templates remain unchanged. Do not advertise them as scientifically verified.

## Per-template requirements

1. Validate that a source actually supports the specific species and behavior; record page/section, evidence type, and limitations.
2. Write distinct preparation, introduction, shaping, generalization, maintenance, and welfare stop conditions where applicable.
3. Record suggested duration as a provisional flexible window or `event_based`/`observation_only`; never fabricate a scientific threshold.
4. Specify measurable success criteria and distinguish product heuristics from published evidence.
5. Attach licensed image assets only after verifying original URL, license, attribution, and reuse rights. Use placeholder/illustration where suitable assets cannot be verified.
6. Keep `reviewStatus: pending_expert_review` until a qualified reviewer signs off.
7. Validate all file hashes and byte sizes before updating manifest in a final content PR.

## Current scope

Evidence registry is a research seed only; 100 template-level source mappings, content rewrites, and image licenses remain outstanding.
