Primary scene: Small pet rat stands fully OUTSIDE beside a VERY roomy ventilated carrier with broad open front and soft bedding, carrier roof three times rat height, full tail visible without intersecting walls. Subject identity: One friendly brown-and-white pet rat, pink ears and paws, one complete long tapered tail. This is a representative rodent illustration, not every rodent species. Reference image is ONLY a warm hand-drawn watercolor/pencil ART STYLE reference, NOT its cat, litter tray or scene; never add a cat or dog. Thin imperfect warm brown outlines, soft pastel sage/lavender accents and subtle colored-pencil watercolor texture. Friendly simplified storybook character proportions, physically coherent anatomy. Caregiver if needed: adult woman, brown hair bun, sage shirt, dark trousers, relaxed posture; full animal and necessary equipment take priority. Square cover, genuinely TRANSPARENT background outside isolated subject and required equipment/enclosure, no room or landscape backdrop, no floor plane. Minimal composition. Whole animal, all paws/hooves, ears/wings and full tail visible with generous padding; enclosure walls never intersect animal body. Voluntary calm participation, no gripping, scruffing, punishment, chasing or force. NO embedded text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, watermarks, arrows, speech bubbles, multiple panels or collage.
