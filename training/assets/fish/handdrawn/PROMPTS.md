## fish_enrichment_object_exploration / cover

Final file: `training/assets/fish/handdrawn/fish_enrichment_object_exploration/cover.png`

Create ONE square transparent PawTrack Fish Enrichment Object Exploration cover. Use reference ONLY for the natural goldfish anatomy and hand-drawn watercolor style. A SMALL orange goldfish, clean side profile, freely swims near a broad smooth pale-lavender CERAMIC ARCH inside a very roomy planted rectangular aquarium. Arch opening at least twice fish body width and height, no sharp edges. Whole fish fully underwater. Natural fish lips, round lateral eye, scales, gill cover and coherent fins/tail. Absolutely NO whiskers, cat muzzle, mammal nose, fur, ears, paws or human face. Fish occupies only 25 percent of tank width, ample free water. Whole tank and fish visible with padding. Transparent background OUTSIDE tank, no human, no room, no feeding ring or food. Thin warm brown pencil outlines, pastel watercolor storybook style, no text letters numbers logos panels or collage.
