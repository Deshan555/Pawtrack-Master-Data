Subject identity: One friendly pink pot-bellied pig with small gray patch on back, natural snout, short curly tail and four cloven hooves. Reference image is ONLY a warm hand-drawn watercolor/pencil ART STYLE reference, NOT its cat, litter tray or scene; never add a cat or dog. Thin imperfect warm brown outlines, soft pastel sage/lavender accents and subtle colored-pencil watercolor texture. Friendly simplified storybook character proportions, physically coherent anatomy. Caregiver if needed: adult woman, brown hair bun, sage shirt, dark trousers, relaxed posture; full animal and necessary equipment take priority. Square cover, genuinely TRANSPARENT background outside isolated subject and required equipment/enclosure, no room or landscape backdrop, no floor plane. Minimal composition. Whole animal, all paws/hooves, ears/wings and full tail visible with generous padding; enclosure walls never intersect animal body.
