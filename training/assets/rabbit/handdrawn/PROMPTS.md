# Rabbit artwork prompts

Tool: built-in image generation, one call per individual asset. The established Cat cover was used solely as a visual style reference, not as an animal or scene reference.

## rabbit_litter_habits / cover

Final file: `training/assets/rabbit/handdrawn/rabbit_litter_habits/cover.png`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: PawTrack rabbit exercise Litter Habits / cover. Primary scene: Rabbit calmly stands completely outside beside a large low-sided lavender litter tray with hay at one side, caregiver sits well away giving space, no waste or forced placement. Subject identity: One friendly cream-and-tan rabbit with TWO upright ears and natural rabbit paws, anatomically coherent. Reference image is ONLY a warm hand-drawn watercolor/pencil ART STYLE reference, NOT its cat, litter tray or scene; never add a cat or dog. Thin imperfect warm brown outlines, soft pastel sage/lavender accents and subtle colored-pencil watercolor texture. Friendly simplified storybook character proportions, physically coherent anatomy. Caregiver if needed: adult woman, brown hair bun, sage shirt, dark trousers, relaxed posture; full animal and necessary equipment take priority. Square cover, genuinely TRANSPARENT background outside isolated subject and required equipment/enclosure, no room or landscape backdrop, no floor plane. Minimal composition. Whole animal, all paws/hooves, ears/wings and full tail visible with generous padding; enclosure walls never intersect animal body. Voluntary calm participation, no gripping, scruffing, punishment, chasing or force. NO embedded text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, watermarks, arrows, speech bubbles, multiple panels or collage.
